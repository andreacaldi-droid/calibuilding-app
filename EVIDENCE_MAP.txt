CALI POWER — EVIDENCE MAP
PMID 35438660 — systematic exercise variation: systematic/anatomical-biomechanical variation can help; random/redundant variation may hinder.
PMID 36662126 — ROM and muscle length: full ROM / long-length work are useful tools; no single universal ROM rule.
PMID 39809454 — preacher vs incline curl: regional elbow-flexor hypertrophy differed by exercise.
PMID 35819335 — overhead vs neutral-arm triceps extension: greater triceps growth, especially long head, in overhead condition.
PMID 38970765 — proximity to failure: hypertrophy tended to improve closer to failure; exact optimum remains uncertain.
PMID 41343037 — volume/frequency dose response: volume shows positive dose response with diminishing returns; frequency mainly distributes dose for hypertrophy.
PMID 34757594 / PMC8891239 — concurrent aerobic + strength compatible with hypertrophy/max strength; explosive strength can be more sensitive.
PMID 36508686 — concurrent training and hypertrophy: whole-muscle hypertrophy not inevitably impaired; HIIT may increase fiber-level interference.
PMID 39930392 — HIFT can improve multiple fitness qualities.
PMID 40560504 — current evidence does not establish Zone 2 as universally optimal.
I90 official public page — useful concepts retained: structured strength + endurance, intervals, aerobic base, metcon, progression.
Not copied as scientific laws: fixed 3 strength + 3 run/week, never same-day, Zone 2 as mandatory/optimal.
