CALI POWER — MASTER SPEC
Primary: hypertrophy + aesthetics.
Secondary: relative strength + control.
Tertiary: athleticism + conditioning + endurance.

Non-negotiable:
- Real history is the only source of truth.
- Session date is fixed at START and survives midnight.
- Atomic close: journal -> history -> localStorage read-back -> clear runner.
- No fixed A-B-C-D.
- No fixed 3+3 strength/run quota.
- Exercise selection uses regions, movement pattern, angle, muscle-length bias, recency, regional overlap,
  progression continuity, joint fit, equipment/location and real time.
- Systematic variation, not random exercise rotation.
- Runner keeps total clock + rest timer + load/reps/RIR/pain/technique.
- POST keeps duration/quality/fatigue/pump/notes.
- Manual history accepts unlimited exercises and does not invent unknown values.
- Forecasts are provisional and never become history.
- Home and travel have equal physiological status.
- Control layer includes support/L-sit/handstand/bodyline/scapular/locomotion options.
- Hybrid layer includes easy aerobic, intervals and strength-biased HIFT.
- I90 contributes concurrent structure, aerobic base, intervals and metcon concepts; Cali Power does not copy
  fixed 3+3 scheduling, mandatory day separation, or treat Zone 2 as universally optimal.
